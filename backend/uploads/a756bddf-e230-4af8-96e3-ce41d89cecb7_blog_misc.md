## Anti-Ragging Policy
**URL:** https://geu.ac.in/anti-ragging/

GEU has a strict zero-tolerance policy on ragging. The anti-ragging cell:
- Monitors campus for any ragging incidents
- Provides a complaints mechanism for students
- Ensures disciplinary action against offenders
- Is aligned with UGC anti-ragging regulations
