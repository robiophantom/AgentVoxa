## Overview

GEU provides on-campus hostel accommodation for both boys and girls. The campus hostels are located within the main campus, bordered by Rajaji National Park and Clement Town Cantonment.

---

## Hostel Facilities

- Separate hostels for boys and girls
- Located on-campus for safety and convenience
- Facilities include:
  - Furnished rooms (single, double, triple sharing options)
  - Mess/cafeteria with nutritious meals
  - High-speed Wi-Fi in hostels
  - Gymnasium access
  - 24/7 security and CCTV surveillance
  - Common rooms and recreational areas
  - Laundry facilities
  - Medical/health facilities on campus

### General Categories (as listed on the site)

Hostel fees typically cover:
- Room rent (per semester/year)
- Mess charges (monthly or per semester)
- Establishment charges
