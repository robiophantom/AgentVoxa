## President

**Prof. (Dr.) Kamal Ghanshala**
- Founder-President of Graphic Era Group of Institutions
- Renowned academician and edupreneur
- Personally teaches Data Structures and Algorithms to every graduating CSE student
- Research contributions acknowledged globally: USA, Canada, UK, France, Germany, Denmark, South Africa, Turkey, Croatia, Thailand
- Awards:
  - **Uttarakhand Ratna, 2007** — for outstanding contribution to Technical and Higher Education
  - **Visionary Edupreneur of India Award, 2017** — conferred by former President of India, Mr. Pranab Mukherjee

## Graphic Era Educational Society (GEES)

**Type:** Non-profit organization  
**Founded:** 1993  
**Purpose:** Provide world-class education and generate resources for quality education  
**Philosophy:** Recognizes the right of every individual to lead a dignified life in a just and equitable manner  
**First institution:** Graphic Era Institute of Technology (GEIT) — first self-financed engineering institute in North India
