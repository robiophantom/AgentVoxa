# Admissions, Scholarships & Placements — Graphic Era Hill University (GEHU)

## 📝 Admission Process

### Overview
GEHU Dehradun admits students to B.Tech CSE and its specializations based on:
1. 12th PCM marks (Merit-based)
2. JEE Main scores
3. GECET (Graphic Era Common Entrance Test) scores
4. CUET scores (where applicable)

---

### Step-by-Step Admission Guide

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Visit https://apply.gehu.ac.in |
| 2 | Fill the online application form |
| 3 | Upload required documents |
| 4 | Appear for GECET (optional but recommended for scholarship) |
| 5 | Receive merit-based admission offer |
| 6 | Pay admission + first semester fee |
| 7 | Report to campus for document verification |
| 8 | Attend orientation and begin classes |

---

### Eligibility Criteria (B.Tech CSE)

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Qualifying Exam** | 12th from recognized board |
| **Mandatory Subjects** | Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry (PCM) |
| **Minimum % (General)** | 50–60% aggregate (AICTE norm) |
| **For Scholarship** | 75%+ in PCM recommended |
| **JEE Main** | Accepted (not mandatory at GEHU) |
| **GECET** | University's own test (preferred for scholarship) |
| **Lateral Entry (Diploma Holders)** | Eligible for 2nd year B.Tech (check latest policy) |

## 🎓 Scholarship Programs

### Merit-Based Scholarships (Academic Performance)

GEHU awards scholarships at admission based on qualifying score and continues them based on annual academic performance:

| Category | Criteria | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Merit Scholarship (Top) | ≥ 95% in 12th PCM / Top JEE tier | Highest slab (up to ₹75,000/year) |
| Merit Scholarship (Mid) | 85–94.99% in 12th PCM | Mid slabs (₹25,000–50,000/year) |
| Merit Scholarship (Entry) | 75–84.99% in 12th PCM | Entry slab (₹15,000–20,000/year) |

### Category-Based Scholarships

| Category | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Uttarakhand Domicile | Up to 25% fee waiver |
| Girl Students | 10% additional on net tuition fee |
| Wards of Military / Paramilitary | 5–10% on tuition fee |
| SC/ST (if applicable) | As per state/central government norms |

### Renewal Criteria

- Maintain **SGPA ≥ 7.0** each semester
- No backlogs (arrear papers)
- Good attendance and conduct

### GECET Scholarship

- Appears in GECET → eligible for additional scholarship tiers
- Higher GECET score = higher scholarship
