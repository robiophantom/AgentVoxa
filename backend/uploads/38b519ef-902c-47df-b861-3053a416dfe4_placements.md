## Overview

Placements are a core focus at GEU. The university has maintained an exceptional track record of consistently high student placements since its establishment. GEU delivers the highest number of Tier-1 placements in the region year-on-year, with maximum selections at global giants like Amazon, Adobe, Microsoft, and Google.

---

## Placement Highlights 2025

Students have secured positions in their dream companies with some of the highest packages in India. The placement ecosystem includes:

- Dedicated and skilled placement trainers working with students from Day 1
- Specialized training for placement-related skills and mindset
- International-level competitive preparation
- A specialized Placement Committee mapping best opportunities to right candidates
