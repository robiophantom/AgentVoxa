## Scholarship Discount Categories (General)

Academic scholarships are frozen for the entire course duration at the bracket obtained at admission. Students must maintain **7.0 SGPA or above** each semester to retain their scholarship.

| Scholarship Bracket | Criteria |
|--------------------|-|
| Merit-based (various %) | Based on qualifying exam score |
| Female Candidate | +10% additional discount |
| Defence Personnel Ward | 5% fee concession |
