## 🏆 Which Should You Choose?

### Choose GEU if:
- You want a **Deemed University** degree (sometimes preferred by employers / for higher studies)
- You're interested in the **research ecosystem**, PhD, or MoUs with top MNCs
- You want **AI & HPC Centre** and dedicated innovation infrastructure
- You have a **high JEE / board score** and want maximum scholarship from GEU
- You want programs like **M.Tech CSE**, **iOS Developer Program**, or Hindi-medium BTech
- You're interested in entrepreneurship via **GEU-TBI**

### Choose GEHU if:
- You are an **Uttarakhand domicile** student (25% fee waiver = major savings)
- You prefer a **closer to home** campus (Haldwani campus option)
- You want to pursue a **Diploma in CSE** first, then lateral entry to B.Tech
- You prefer **slightly lower one-time admission fees**
- The **Dehradun campus** suits your location preference equally

### Both Are Equal In:
- NAAC A+ accreditation
- Top company placements
- GECET-based scholarship system
- Core CSE, AI, ML, and Cybersecurity specializations
- Faculty quality (IIT/NIT educated)
- Campus facilities and student life
