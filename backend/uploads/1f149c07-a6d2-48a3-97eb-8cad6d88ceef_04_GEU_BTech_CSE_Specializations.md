# B.Tech CSE Specializations — Graphic Era (Deemed to be University)

## Overview

GEU's CSE Department offers multiple B.Tech specializations in addition to the core B.Tech CSE program. These are designed to equip students with focused expertise in high-demand domains.

---

## 1️⃣ B.Tech CSE — Core (No Specialization)

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Program Name** | B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering |
| **Duration** | 4 Years / 8 Semesters |
| **Degree** | B.Tech CSE |
| **Fee Link** | https://geu.ac.in/fee/btech-cse |
| **Intake** | Regular |
| **Eligibility** | 12th PCM, min 75% recommended for scholarship |

### Focus Areas
A broad-spectrum computer science program covering programming, systems, networking, databases, operating systems, software engineering, and introductory AI/ML.
