**Source:** https://geu.ac.in/university-life/

## About the Campus

The oldest and most elaborate campus of Graphic Era is situated in the scenic valley of Dehradun. The campus is bordered by:
- **Rajaji National Park** on one side
- **Clement Town Cantonment** on the other

The campus sets the benchmark for all universities and educational institutes in the region.

### Academic Facilities
- State-of-the-art lecture theatres with Smart Boards
- Multiple computer centres (3,590+ updated computers)
- World-class libraries
- High-speed Wi-Fi across the entire campus
- Research laboratories (department-specific)
- Indoor and outdoor auditoriums

## GRAFEST — Annual Campus Festival

**GRAFEST** is one of India's biggest campus fests, hosted at Graphic Era annually.

**Features:**
- International-level celebrity performances
- Technical events and competitions
- Cultural events (music, dance, drama)
- Sports competitions
- Entrepreneurship events
- Gaming and e-sports
- Workshops and seminars
- Food stalls and exhibitions

GRAFEST attracts thousands of students from colleges across India and has featured top national performers and celebrities.
