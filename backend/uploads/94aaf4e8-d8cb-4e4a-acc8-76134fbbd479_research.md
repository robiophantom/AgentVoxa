## Philosophy

GEU believes knowledge-driven growth fueled by innovation is essential for a globalized society. The university focuses equally on teaching and research excellence, offering state-of-the-art infrastructure to support scientific studies and academic research in frontier areas.

## Technology Business Incubator (TBI-GEU)

**Website:** https://tbi.geu.ac.in  
**Founded:** 2015  
**Sponsored by:** India's Department of Science and Technology (DST)  
**Status:** Uttarakhand's first recognized incubator  

**Mission:** Nurture entrepreneurship and innovation by offering:
- Office facilities and infrastructure
- Training and mentoring programs
- Networking with investors and industry
- Support for startups and SMEs
- Contribution to regional economic development
