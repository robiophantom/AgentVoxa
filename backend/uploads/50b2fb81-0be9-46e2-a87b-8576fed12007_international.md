# International Programs — Graphic Era (Deemed to be University)
**Source:** https://geu.ac.in/international/

---

## Overview

GEU has tie-ups with top universities worldwide, opening doors to global opportunities for students. Various international programs allow students to earn international exposure, academic credits abroad, and even full degrees through collaborative arrangements.

---

## International Program Formats

| Program Type | Structure |
|-------------|-----------|
| 3+1+1 | 3 years at GEU + 1 year abroad + 1 year (Masters) abroad |
| 3+1 | 3 years at GEU + 1 year abroad |
| 2+2 | 2 years at GEU + 2 years abroad |
| 1+1 | 1 year at GEU + 1 year abroad (PG level) |

---

## International Opportunities Available

- **Semester Exchange Programs** — study one or more semesters at partner universities
- **Credit Transfer** — international credits recognized toward GEU degree
- **Internships Abroad** — paid/unpaid internships at international companies/universities
- **Joint / Dual Degrees** — earn a degree from both GEU and a partner university
- **Research Collaborations** — work with international research teams

## NBA Accreditation Benefit for International Studies

GEU has 6 NBA-accredited programs. NBA accreditation allows GEU students to:
- Apply directly to top universities abroad without equivalency hurdles
- Transfer credits seamlessly to international institutions
- Pursue Masters/PhD programs abroad with recognized qualifications
