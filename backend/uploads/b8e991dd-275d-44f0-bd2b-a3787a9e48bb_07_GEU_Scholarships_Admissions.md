# Scholarships & Admissions — Graphic Era (Deemed to be University)

## 🎓 Scholarship Programs

GEU offers extensive merit-based and category-based scholarships, making it one of the most affordable top-ranked universities in Uttarakhand.

---

### Merit-Based Academic Scholarships (B.Tech CSE — Per Year)

These are awarded at the time of admission based on qualifying exam performance:

| Qualifying Score | Annual Scholarship |
|---|---|
| 12th PCM ≥ 95% OR JEE Main ≥ 99 %ile | ₹75,000/year |
| 12th PCM 92.5–94.99% OR JEE 98.5–98.99 %ile | ₹50,000/year |
| 12th PCM 90–92.49% OR JEE 97–98.49 %ile | ₹35,000/year |
| 12th PCM 87.5–89.99% OR JEE 94–96.99 %ile | ₹30,000/year |
| 12th PCM 85–87.49% OR JEE 92–93.99 %ile | ₹25,000/year |
| 12th PCM 80–84.99% OR JEE 90–91.99 %ile | ₹20,000/year |
| 12th PCM 75–79.99% OR JEE 85–89.99 %ile | ₹15,000/year |

> CUET scores are also accepted as qualifying criteria (equivalent NTA score ranges apply).

---

### Category-Based Additional Scholarships

| Category | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Girl Students** | Additional 10% on net tuition fee |
| **Wards of Defence Personnel** | Additional 5% on tuition fee |

---

### GECET Scholarship

- **GECET** = Graphic Era Common Entrance Test (University's own entrance)
- Taking GECET and performing well can unlock additional scholarship tiers
- Exam held typically in January–February each year

---

### Availability

- **25% of all seats** are reserved for scholarship categories
- Scholarships are granted on a **first-come-first-serve** basis within each merit band
- Scholarships are typically renewed each year subject to academic performance (usually SGPA ≥ 7 without backlogs)

## 📝 Admission Process

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Visit** https://apply.geu.ac.in
2. **Fill** the online application form
3. **Submit** qualifying exam details (12th marks / JEE / CUET scores)
4. **Provisional Admission** — For early applicants, seats are allocated based on projected scores derived from 10th percentage
5. **Counselling** — Merit-based seat allocation
6. **Document Verification** — Submit original documents at the campus
7. **Fee Payment** — Pay admission fee, enrolment fee, and first semester fee
8. **Classes Begin** — As per Academic Calendar

---

### Required Documents

- 10th Marksheet & Certificate
- 12th Marksheet (provisional / final)
- JEE Main Scorecard (if applicable)
- CUET Scorecard (if applicable)
- Transfer Certificate (TC)
- Character Certificate
- Migration Certificate (if from another state board)
- Passport-size photographs
- ID Proof (Aadhaar Card / PAN / Passport)
- Category Certificate (for Defence personnel wards, SC/ST/OBC if applicable)
