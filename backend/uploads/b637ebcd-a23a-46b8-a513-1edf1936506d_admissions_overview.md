# Admissions — Graphic Era (Deemed to be University)
**Source:** https://geu.ac.in/admissions/

---

## Overview

Graphic Era offers 85+ UG, PG and Doctoral programmes across domains:
- Engineering
- Computer Application
- Management
- Commerce
- Life Sciences
- Food Technology
- Hospitality
- Paramedical
- Humanities and Social Sciences

**Admission basis:** Strictly merit-based on qualifying examination marks.  
**Process:** Fully available online.

> ⚠️ GEU does NOT outsource admission activities. Beware of imposters.  
> Contact: **enquiry@geu.ac.in** | Toll Free: **1800 890 6027**

---

## Why Choose GEU?

1. **Top Placements** — Consistently high placements at Amazon, Adobe, Microsoft, Google, and more
2. **Merit-Based Scholarships** — Available in all courses for entire course duration
3. **Additional Scholarships** — 10% extra for female candidates; 5% fee concession for wards of Defence Personnel
4. **Top Accreditations** — NAAC A+, NBA, NIRF Top 50 (exclusive in the region)
