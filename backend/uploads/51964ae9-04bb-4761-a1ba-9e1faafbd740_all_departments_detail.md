## Mechanical Engineering
**URL:** https://geu.ac.in/mechanical-engineering/
**Established:** Early years of GEU

### About
Industry-integrated B.Tech, M.Tech, and PhD programs. Key partnership with **TATA Technologies Ltd.** to build next-generation engineering professionals in Mechanical, Automobile, Robotics, and Automation disciplines.

### Programs
- B.Tech Mechanical Engineering (4 years)
- B.Tech ME (Hons.) – Robotics and Automation
- B.Tech ME (Hons.) – Automobile specialization
- B.Tech ME with minor in CSE
- M.Tech Mechanical Engineering (2 years)
- PhD Mechanical Engineering

### Focus Areas
- Thermodynamics and fluid mechanics
- Manufacturing and production engineering
- Robotics and automation
- CAD/CAM and design
- Automobile engineering
- Electric vehicles

### NBA Accreditation
B.Tech Mechanical Engineering is **NBA accredited**.
