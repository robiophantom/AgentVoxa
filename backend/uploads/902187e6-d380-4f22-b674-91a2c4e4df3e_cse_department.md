## About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Graphic Era (Deemed to be University) celebrates 25 years of excellence in research and education. It has shaped the careers of distinguished alumni who are now prize-winning researchers, developers, and project leaders at world-class multinational organizations including Microsoft, Yahoo, Amazon, Google, Intel, Oracle, Adobe, and Cognizant.
