## History & Founding

Graphic Era (Deemed to be University) is the culmination of the vision of its founder, Prof. (Dr.) Kamal Ghanshala. In 1993, he began with ₹29,000 and a mission to transform higher education in the Doon Valley, starting with Graphic Era Computer Centre — developing software and training on Computer Graphics. In 1997, this evolved into Graphic Era Institute of Technology (GEIT), the first self-financed engineering institute in North India.

In 2008, following an outstanding placement record and academic excellence, the Institute was granted Deemed University status under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956 (Notification F.9-48/2007-U.3(A), dated 14 August 2008), approved by the Ministry of Human Resource Development, Government of India.

### President
**Prof. (Dr.) Kamal Ghanshala**
