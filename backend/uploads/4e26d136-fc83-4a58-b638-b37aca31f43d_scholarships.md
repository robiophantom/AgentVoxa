## Overview

GEU offers handsome scholarships that have set trends among private universities in India. Scholarships apply across all courses and are defined in multiple categories.

---

## Types of Scholarships & Waivers

### 1. Academic Scholarship

- Defined clearly in the Fee Structure for all courses
- Categorized based on qualifying examination merit at the time of admission
- **Frozen for the entire course duration** — scholarship bracket does not change
- **Condition:** Candidate must score a minimum aggregate of **7.0 SGPA** each semester
- If SGPA falls below 7.0 in any semester, the scholarship is NOT applied for that semester
- Scholarship resumes in subsequent semesters if SGPA is restored to 7.0 or above

### 2. Additional Scholarship for Female Candidates

- **10% additional scholarship** on top of merit scholarship for female candidates

### 3. Defence Personnel Ward Concession

- **5% fee concession** for wards of serving/retired Defence Personnel

### 4. Other Waivers

- Various waivers may be available; detailed in the fee structure document for each course

---

## How to Retain Scholarship

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Minimum SGPA | 7.0 aggregate each semester |
| Valid for | Entire course duration |
| What happens if SGPA < 7 | Scholarship suspended for that semester only |
| Restoration | Scholarship applies again once SGPA ≥ 7.0 |
