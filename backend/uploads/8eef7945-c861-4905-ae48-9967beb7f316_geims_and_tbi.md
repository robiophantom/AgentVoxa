## GEIMS — Graphic Era Institute of Medical Sciences

**Website:** https://geims.geu.ac.in  
**Type:** Medical education and healthcare institution  
**Affiliated with:** Graphic Era Group of Institutions

### Mission
To nurture healthcare leaders and advance medical excellence through state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and innovative programs.

### About
GEIMS is dedicated to excellence in:
- Medical education
- Research in healthcare and clinical sciences
- Healthcare delivery and community outreach

### Key Features
- State-of-the-art clinical and research facilities
- Experienced faculty from leading medical institutions
- Integration of research with clinical practice
- Community health outreach programs
- Commitment to academic rigor and clinical excellence

### Programs Offered
GEIMS prepares students for careers in medicine and healthcare. Specific MBBS and postgraduate medical programs are available — see https://geims.geu.ac.in for current program details.

### Research at GEIMS
Conducts healthcare research and contributes to advancements in medicine through:
- Clinical research
- Community health studies
- Collaborative research with GEU departments (Biotechnology, Biosciences, etc.)

## TBI-GEU — Technology Business Incubator

**Website:** https://tbi.geu.ac.in  
**Founded:** 2015  
**Sponsored by:** Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India  
**Status:** Uttarakhand's **first recognized incubator**

### Mission
To nurture entrepreneurship and innovation in the region by supporting startups and SMEs through:
- Office space and infrastructure
- Training and capacity-building programs
- Mentorship from industry experts
- Networking with investors
- Access to government schemes and grants

### Services Offered

| Service | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Office/Workspace | Dedicated incubation space for startups |
| Infrastructure | Labs, equipment, high-speed internet |
| Training | Workshops on entrepreneurship, funding, IP, marketing |
| Mentoring | Expert mentors from industry and academia |
| Funding Support | Guidance for seed funding, angel investment, govt grants |
| Networking | Connections to investors, corporates, and peer startups |

### Focus Sectors
- Technology (software, AI, IoT)
- Biotechnology and healthcare
- Clean energy and sustainability
- Agriculture technology
- EdTech
- Manufacturing and engineering

### Impact
Supports both student entrepreneurs from GEU and external startup founders from the region, contributing to the economic development of Uttarakhand.

### Relationship with GEU
TBI-GEU is tightly integrated with GEU's academic ecosystem:
- Students can get mentored and incubated through GEU-TBI
- Courses on entrepreneurship and innovation are embedded in GEU's curriculum
- Faculty and industry experts serve as TBI mentors
- TBI connects students with alumni who have become successful entrepreneurs

## GEU Online Education

**Website:** https://geuonline.com  
**Type:** Online degree programs  
**Distinction:** First university in Uttarakhand to offer UGC/AICTE-entitled online education (launched 2022)

### Programs Available
- Online UG programs
- Online PG programs

### Platform Features
- State-of-the-art online content delivery system
- Online assessment and examination
- Industry-relevant curriculum
- Designed for working professionals
- Flexible learning schedule

### Target Audience
Corporate professionals and working adults who want to pursue higher education without disrupting their careers.
