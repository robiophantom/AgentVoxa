## 📋 Eligibility Criteria

- Must have passed **Class XII (10+2)** from a recognized board
- Compulsory subjects: **Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry** (PCM)
- As per AICTE guidelines
- Admission based on merit (12th PCM percentage / JEE Main / CUET scores)
- Minimum: **75% aggregate in PCM** (12th standard)
- No minimum percentage mandated by AICTE, but GEU scholarship slabs start from 75%

---

## 📝 Admission Procedure

| Step | Details |
|---|---|
| **Mode** | Online / Merit-based |
| **Accepted Scores** | 12th PCM %, JEE Main Percentile, CUET NTA Score |
| **Provisional Admission** | Based on projected 12th scores (calculated from 10th percentage) — only for meritorious students |
| **JEE Mandatory?** | No — private university accepts merit-based admissions without JEE |
| **Entrance Exam (Own)** | GECET (Graphic Era Common Entrance Test) — for scholarship eligibility |
| **Apply Link** | https://apply.geu.ac.in |

## 🗓️ Program Structure

The program spans **4 years divided into 8 semesters**. Students take:

1. **Core Courses** — Mandatory foundational subjects
2. **Elective Courses** — Specialization-oriented subjects
3. **Laboratory Courses** — Hands-on practical sessions
4. **Project Work** — Minor and Major projects

---

| Subject | Description |
|---|---|

## 🎯 Program Outcomes (POs)

Upon successful completion, graduates will be able to:

1. Apply engineering fundamentals and computer science principles to solve complex problems
2. Design and develop software systems, algorithms, and data structures
3. Analyze and evaluate technical problems with modern computing tools
4. Work effectively in multidisciplinary teams
5. Communicate technical concepts clearly in oral and written form
6. Engage in lifelong learning and adapt to evolving technologies
7. Understand and apply ethical responsibilities in engineering practice
8. Demonstrate an awareness of societal and environmental impact of technology
9. Manage engineering projects with awareness of business and project management norms
10. Apply domain knowledge to contribute to research and innovation
