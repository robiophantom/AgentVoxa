# B.Tech CSE — Semester-wise Syllabus & Curriculum Guide
## (Applicable to GEU & GEHU — General Structure)

> Note: The exact syllabus may vary slightly between GEU and GEHU, and between specializations. This represents the standard structure for B.Tech CSE in India, aligned with AICTE guidelines and university curriculum.

---

|---|---|
| Mathematics I (Calculus, Linear Algebra) | Core |

## 📅 Semester 7 — Industry Internship + Advanced Electives

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Industrial Training / Internship** | 6–8 weeks (typically done in Summer after Sem 6, reported in Sem 7) |
| Advanced Elective 1 | Domain-specific |
| Advanced Elective 2 | Domain-specific |
| Major Project Part I | Start of final year project |
| Technical Communication | Report writing, presentations |
| Seminar / Research Talk | Presentation on emerging topics |

**Key Activities:** Many students secure pre-placement offers (PPOs) via internships in this semester.

---

## 📅 Semester 8 — Final Year Project + Electives

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Major Project Part II** | Full-term capstone project |
| Advanced Elective 3 | Domain-specific |
| Advanced Elective 4 | Open elective (cross-domain) |
| Comprehensive Viva | Final oral examination |
| Industrial Expert Lecture Series | Optional |

**Key Activities:** Campus placements, final project submission, viva, graduation.

## 📊 CGPA & Credit System

| Metric | Standard |
|---|---|
| Credit System | Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) |
| Credits per Semester | 20–25 credits |
| Total Credits (4 Years) | ~160–180 credits |
| Grading | 10-point CGPA scale |
| Pass Marks | Typically 40–45% in each subject |
| Backlog Policy | Students with backlogs may have scholarships affected |
| Grade Points | O (10), A+ (9), A (8), B+ (7), B (6), C (5), F (Fail) |
